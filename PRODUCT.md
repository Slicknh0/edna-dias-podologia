# Edna Dias Podologia

## What this is

A one-person podology practice in Vila Medeiros, north zone of São Paulo. Edna Dias
treats the feet people are embarrassed to show — ingrown nails, calluses, fissures,
fungus — with declared specialist attention to diabetic feet. The entire business runs
through one WhatsApp conversation: there is no booking system, no price list online,
no patient portal.

**Unique mechanism:** a named, reachable professional a few streets away who will look
at your foot this week, and specialist attention for the diabetic feet that general
salons decline to touch.

## The surface being built

A single landing page. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The only conversion is a WhatsApp
message. Every other outcome is a failure.

## Audience and scene

Someone at home, at night, phone in hand, foot hurting right now. Or an adult child
worried about a diabetic parent's feet and unwilling to trust a random salon. They
arrive from Google Maps, Instagram, or a neighbor's recommendation, on a mid-range
Android phone, on mobile data.

They are not shopping. They are deciding whether this person is real, competent, close
by, and safe to message.

Primary: residents of Vila Medeiros and adjacent north-zone neighborhoods; adults and
older adults; people in active foot discomfort; people with diabetes seeking someone
who will take their feet seriously; family members arranging care for a parent.

## What must be believed

1. She is a real professional, not an improvised service.
2. Her room is clean and her instruments are handled properly.
3. Diabetic feet are genuinely within her competence, not an upsell.
4. Messaging her is easy, immediate, and low-commitment.

## Hard constraints

**Uninventable.** No certifications, no formal training, no years of experience, no
patient counts, no awards, no insurance/convênios, no prices, no opening hours, no
testimonials, no before/after imagery, no clinical promises, no statistics. Where a
fact is missing, omit it or route the visitor to WhatsApp.

**Medical honesty.** No diagnosis, no cure claims, no promise of prevention. Diabetic
foot copy describes attention and care, never outcomes.

**No stranger's face.** No stock person may stand in for Edna. Her photographs were not
supplied and could not be retrieved from her public profiles (Instagram serves a login
wall; Facebook returns HTTP 400). The page ships with designed photo slots and a
manifest of what to drop in.

**Assumption, labeled:** the Google rating (5,0 / 33) is carried from the brief because
Google blocked automated access to the profile. If the count has moved, the page has a
single place to change it.

## Constraints of build

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS — chosen by the user. No framework, no build step, no
dependency install. The page must open by double-clicking `index.html` and deploy to
any static host. Portuguese (pt-BR) only.

## What success looks like

A visitor in pain understands what this is, believes she is competent and nearby, and
opens WhatsApp — within seconds, on a phone, one-handed.

## Brand commitment (standing preference)

The user was offered a dealt visual direction and chose **the category standard,
played straight**, then set the quality bar at **premium clinic websites**: large
confident typography, generous whitespace, photography as protagonist, sober green,
studio-level finish on a conventional structure.

This is a durable commitment, not a one-off. Future surfaces inherit it: conventional
clinic-landing arrangement, executed at branding-studio craft. No irony, no smuggled
quirk. The brief's anti-generic constraints still bind — identical card grids as page
structure, three-cards-per-section, heavy shadows, and emoji-as-icon are out.
